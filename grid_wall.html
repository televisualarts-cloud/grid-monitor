Choose this screen's layout

Each browser window picks its own layout, so for two screens open this page twice and choose screen 1 on one and screen 2 on the other. Clicking a substation on one screen shows its detail on the other. Press M to come back here, F for full screen.

Layouts are drawn at the exact pixel size and scaled to fit the window. On screen 1 of a dual layout, “start screen 2” opens the other screen full screen on your second display (Chrome/Edge ask permission once).
