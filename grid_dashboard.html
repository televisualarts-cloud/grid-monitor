OpenWeather API key

OpenWeather powers the local weather panel and the rainfall nowcast. An optional One Call 4.0 subscription unlocks the offshore rain watch and the minute-ahead nowcast; without it the free Current Weather Data (2.5) tier is used automatically. Paste your API key below — it's stored by the local server (in openweather_key.json) and used only to fetch weather, never shown in the page again. Calls are throttled to 300 per day to stay within your OpenWeather budget.

Where do I find my key?

New OpenWeather keys can take up to ~2 hours to activate. If you see a 401 just after signing up, that's why.

Audible & spoken alarms

Sound is off until you arm it (browsers block audio until you interact with the page). Alarms fire once when a condition first appears, never on stale data, and never on the first reading after load. Critical alarms repeat until the condition clears.

muted

Arming resets each time you reload the page. Your category choices are remembered. River and rainfall alarms only work while the EA panel has loaded nearby data, repeat at most hourly, and clear automatically when you change location.

GB Generators

metered fuel totals · station split from notifications on-grid — · — stations
Fuel-group sizes are metered (Elexon FUELINST; solar is the Sheffield PVLive embedded estimate). Within each group, stations are split by their notified share (PN) and scaled to the metered group total — so a station's MW is its share of real output, not an independent meter reading. Embedded solar has no per-station data, so it's one estimated tile. Pumped storage shows only when generating.
