Unplanned cuts only. Faults include every operator (SPEN too); customers-off excludes SPEN, which publishes no customer counts. Cuts are binned by the trailing-max gust (~1 h) of the region's METAR stations, so a flat low-wind end is the calm baseline, not a signal.

Customers off by operator

Stacked bands: the top edge is the national total. Largest operator over the window at the bottom. A band stops where that operator's feed was down — nothing is filled in; hover shows "no data". SPEN publishes no customer counts, so it has no band. Strip under the plot: grid events (red critical/severe, amber warning). Window and stepped/raw follow the main page.

Feeds & API keys

NGED, UK Power Networks and Northern Powergrid are open — no key needed. Electricity North West, SP Energy Networks and NIE Networks (Northern Ireland) need a free OpenDataSoft key. ESB Networks (Republic of Ireland) works on a shared key by default — leave it blank unless you want to override it. Keys are stored by the local server only and never shown here again.
